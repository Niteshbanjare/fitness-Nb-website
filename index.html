FITNESS KNOWLEDGE

LEARN BEFORE
YOU TRAIN.

Simple fitness concepts that can help beginners understand training better.

Why Warm Up?

A gradual warm-up can prepare you for movement and help you transition into your training session.

Why Recovery Matters

Training is only one part of progress. Rest, sleep and adequate nutrition also support adaptation.

Progressive Improvement

Instead of rushing, gradually improve your repetitions, control, technique or training difficulty.

Consistency

A realistic routine that you can maintain is more useful than constantly chasing extreme challenges.

Technique First

Learn the movement and control it before trying to make the exercise harder.

Listen To Your Body

Normal training effort and pain are not the same thing. Stop and get appropriate help if something feels wrong.
